# EnterCalc Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 8th 2026

## Overview

EnterCalc respects your privacy.

EnterCalc does not collect, transmit, share, sell, rent, or disclose personal information or other user data.

Specifically, EnterCalc:

- Does not require an account or sign-in.
- Does not collect personal information.
- Does not use analytics, advertising, tracking technologies, or telemetry.
- Does not use third-party crash reporting services.
- Does not upload calculator inputs, calculation history, settings, clipboard contents, or other app data to external servers.

## On-Device Data

To provide app functionality, EnterCalc may store information locally on your device, including:

- Calculation history
- App settings and preferences
- Theme and display preferences

This information remains on your device and is not transmitted by EnterCalc.

## Children's Privacy

EnterCalc does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

If EnterCalc's data practices change in a future release, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those changes take effect.
