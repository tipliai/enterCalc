# EnterCalc Support

Last updated: May 16, 2026

EnterCalc support is available by email and through the public GitHub repository.

## Contact

- Support email: [support@tipli.ai](mailto:support@tipli.ai)
- GitHub issues: [github.com/tipliai/enterCalc/issues](https://github.com/tipliai/enterCalc/issues)
- Source repository: [github.com/tipliai/enterCalc](https://github.com/tipliai/enterCalc)

When reporting a problem, include your device model, OS version, app version, and the steps needed to reproduce the issue.

## Basic App Requirements

- iPhone and iPad require iOS 17.0 or later.
- Mac requires macOS 14.0 or later.

## Privacy

The public privacy policy is available at [tipliai.github.io/enterCalc/privacy-policy](https://tipliai.github.io/enterCalc/privacy-policy/).
